HomeSpend & Grocery

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to HomeSpend & Grocery. This application helps users manage personal expenses, income, grocery purchases, and borrowed or lent money. We respect your privacy and are committed to handling your information responsibly. This Privacy Policy explains what information the application handles and how it is used.

2. Information We Store

Depending on the features you use, the application may store information entered by you, including:

  • Daily expenses and income records.
  • Expense categories and transaction descriptions.
  • Grocery lists, item names, quantities and prices.
  • Borrowed and lent money records.
  • Notes and transaction details.
  • App preferences, themes and language settings.

3. Data Storage

Your financial and grocery information is stored locally on your device using the application's local database.

If you use optional backup features, selected application data may also be stored in your authorized Google Drive account.

4. Google Account and Google Drive Backup

The application may offer Google account authentication and Google Drive backup features. These features are used only when you choose to connect your Google account and grant the required permissions.

If you enable Google Drive backup, application backup files may be stored in your Google Drive account to help you restore your records when reinstalling the application or changing devices.

Google account information and permissions are handled according to Google's applicable privacy policies and authorization procedures.

5. How We Use Information

Information handled by the application is used to provide its features, including:

  • Recording and managing daily expenses.
  • Tracking income and monthly financial summaries.
  • Managing grocery lists and purchases.
  • Tracking borrowed and lent money.
  • Generating reports and exporting records.
  • Providing backup and restore functionality when enabled.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal financial or grocery records.

Information may be processed by service providers when necessary to provide features you choose to use, such as Google authentication, Google Drive backup or advertising services, if included in the application.

7. Advertising and Third-Party Services

If advertising services are integrated into the application, third-party advertising providers may collect certain information according to their own privacy policies.

Third-party services operate under their own terms and privacy policies. Users should review those policies where applicable.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled by the application. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users are responsible for maintaining the security of their devices and Google accounts.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Information stored locally remains on your device until it is deleted through available application controls or by clearing the application's data or uninstalling the application.

Backup files stored in Google Drive may need to be deleted separately through the user's Google Drive account.

10. Children's Privacy

HomeSpend & Grocery is not specifically designed for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in this age group.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when application features or privacy practices change. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised update date.
